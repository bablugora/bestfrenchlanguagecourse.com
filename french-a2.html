Course Overview

French A2 level un students ke liye hai jo basic A1 complete kar chuke hain. Is elementary course mein aap basic sentences se aage badhkar complex conversation seekhte hain. Aap past events ke baare mein baat karna, kisi situation ko detail mein describe karna, aur opinions express karna seekhenge. Yeh level aapko practical fluency ki taraf le jata hai.

What You Will Learn (Syllabus)

  • Past Tenses: Mastering Passé Composé and Imparfait structures
  • Describing your past life, memories, habits, and background stories
  • Advanced Situations: Booking hotels, making travel plans, and medical discussions
  • Grammar expansion: Relative pronouns, future tense, and comparative structures
  • DELF A2 structured speaking, listening, and reading mocks
