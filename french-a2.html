Course Overview

French A2 level un students ke liye hai jo basic A1 complete kar chuke hain. Is elementary course mein aap basic sentences se aage badhkar complex conversation seekhte hain. Aap past events (beete kal) ke baare mein baat karna, kisi situation ko describe karna, aur express opinions dena seekhenge. Yeh level aapko functional fluency ki taraf le jata hai.

What You Will Learn (Syllabus)

  • Past Tenses: Mastering Passé Composé and Imparfait structures
  • Describing your past life, memories, habits, and background stories
  • Advanced Situations: Booking hotels, making travel plans, and medical discussions
  • Grammar expansion: Relative pronouns, future tense, and comparative structures
  • DELF A2 special structured speaking, listening, and reading mocks
