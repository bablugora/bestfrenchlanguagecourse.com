Course Overview

French B1 level ko sach mein "Job Level" kaha jata hai kyunki yeh aapko corporate companies aur MNCs mein entry dilwata hai. Is level mein aap basic conversations se upar uthkar fluidly baat karna seekhte hain. Aap corporate presentations manage karna, professional formal emails likhna, aur opinions ko articulate karna seekhenge. B1 poora karne ke baad aap kisi French-speaking workspace mein confidently survive kar sakte hain.

What You Will Learn (Syllabus)

  • Advanced Moods: Mastering Le Subjonctif and Conditionnel structures
  • Professional Writing: Drafting formal business cover letters and corporate emails
  • Abstract Debates: Expressing points of view, agreement, and disagreement on social topics
  • Complex Syntax: Hypotheses, relative pronouns (dont, lequel), and reported speech
  • DELF B1 Mock Exams: Rigorous practice for text analysis and oral exposition
