Course Overview

French A1 level bilkul zero se shuru hota hai. Agar aapko French ka pehla shabd bhi nahi pata, toh yeh course aapke liye design kiya gaya hai. Isme aapko basic greeting, alphabet, counting aur normal daily life ke sentences bolna aur samajhna sikhaya jata hai. Yeh course complete karne ke baad aap confidence ke sath daily conversation kar payenge.

What You Will Learn (Syllabus)

  • French Alphabets, Pronunciation & Counting
  • How to introduce yourself and others (Formal & Informal)
  • Basic Grammar: Present Tense, Articles, Nouns & Adjectives
  • Daily Conversations: Asking for directions, ordering food, shopping
  • Mock Tests & Exam Practice for International DELF A1 Exam
